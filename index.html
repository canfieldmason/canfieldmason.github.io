Replaced a drifted legacy equation controlling how much carpet goes on every roll at a plant running 26M+ ft a year. 500+ rolls measured across 89 styles, wind targets validated with control charts and process capability. $44.8K annual savings, 2–3× the model it replaced.

EarlyEar — Cattle Health Monitor

A low-power ear tag for early detection of Bovine Respiratory Disease. Rebuilt around the power budget that killed the first prototype: 90 mA down to 3 mA, four hours of field life out to four days, every stage measured on a shunt rig built to read microamps on a milliamp meter.
